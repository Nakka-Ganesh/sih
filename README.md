# 📞 DialIQ AI

> **Bridging citizens and government services through intelligent, voice-first AI conversations.**

DialIQ AI is a smart voice platform that lets everyday citizens access government schemes, check eligibility, and complete official procedures simply by speaking over a phone call—eliminating confusing portals and long queues.

---

## 💡 The Problem & The Solution

- **The Problem:** Government portals and paperwork can be complicated, overwhelming, and inaccessible for non-tech-savvy citizens, rural populations, or those facing language barriers.
- **Our Solution:** DialIQ AI turns complex public service processes into a natural, spoken conversation. Citizens dial in, ask questions in their own language, and receive instant, step-by-step guidance or direct service execution.

---

## 🌟 Key Capabilities

- **🗣️ Natural Voice Conversations:** Talk naturally using everyday speech—no robotic keypress menus (IVR) or technical jargon required.
- **📋 Scheme & Eligibility Guidance:** Instantly learn what government schemes you qualify for, what documents you need, and how to apply.
- **⚡ Real-Time Integration:** Connects securely with official databases and department APIs to retrieve status updates and process requests live.
- **🔒 Privacy & Trust First:** Built with enterprise-grade data encryption, consent-based verification, and strict role-based access.
